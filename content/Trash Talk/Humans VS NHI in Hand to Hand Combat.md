Occasionally, I consider the NHI culture and how they would view combat. I reference our own culture for normalization, and it looks like even the richest of humans still place it on a pedestal of respect. Zuckerberg is an example of this, as he took up Brazilian jiu jitsu, competed publicly, and showed interest in fighting Elon Musk. His leg was severely broken during training, and he recovered and got back to it. Given this angle, I think it's possible that NHI still have interest in hand to hand combat, though it's largely irrelevant.
