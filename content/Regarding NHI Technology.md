This is a discussion surrounding observed NHI technology and extrapolation to avenues where it could also be used. Having observed NHI technology myself, I'm uniquely qualified to give information on this topic. The NHI monitored me in my youth at close range. They later enslaved me using their 'neural link' technology. I'm going to talk about the technologies I've seen employed and also extrapolate to other uses. Reapplication of fundamentals.

I'll start with what I know the most about, and that's their **neural link** technology. This technology has incredible use potential. In it's given form, it allows biologics to be read in real-time. Their emotions and thoughts are delivered to the NHI. This **can be used silently** to gather information or it can be used for two-way communication. It grants the NHI the ability to use the organism's internal voice. This can be used in combination with the silent gathering approach to subtly direct the organism, as it may not recognize the foreign nature of the replacement.

Neural link also grants the NHI the ability to take some **control of the biologic's muscle function**. The NHI seem to prioritize gaining control of the mouth for speech purposes, in the case of humans, as it's one of the first mechanisms they demonstrated control of. They can exert control over any part of the organism, which can be resisted to a degree. When resisting and while they attempt control, a tingling in the muscle can be felt, similar to low level tickling.

Control of the nervous system allows the NHI to **stimulate pleasure or pain in targeted areas**. Agony can be induced, as well as high pleasure. An example of agony would be 'burning' the spinal cord to cause severe pain. This activates nearby muscles into contraction and cramping. I was left under this effect for about two years where I was yelling in agony through exhausted sleep. The incredible and constant pain is severely disabling both physically and mentally.

In addition to the burning spinal cord, they direct what feels like electrical strikes into muscles at will, causing severe electrical stabbing-like pain. A specific muscle would be targeted for some time with repeated strikes, followed by moving to a different area and remaining for some time before moving on. These strikes are quick and unpredictable. While under this effect and yelling out in high pain in public, many people would respond with laughter. 

As previously mentioned, the technology also allows them to stimulate pleasure at targeted locations, which can be multiple locations at once. This could be considered positive upon first hearing of it, however, when used incessantly, it becomes another form of torture. It can also be used as a threat because it can be used in public to make a person cry out in pleasure. The technology allows sexual predation and rape which can't be avoided as there is no one to push away or stop.

Large static shocks are in their tool belt. To gain my attention I was struck on top of the head twice in a row in exactly the same spot. This was meant to be interpreted as a "knock knock" and force me to engage with them. With the strength of the shocks, I believe they could likely kill with a single discharge to a main hub internally, such as the hypothalamus or heart.
