## Introduction

These are my observations surrounding NHI technology. The NHI approached me in my youth at close range. They later enslaved me using their 'neural link' technology and forced me to research or deal with attacks if I didn't. I'm going to talk about the technologies I've seen employed.

## Neural Link

I'll start with what I know the most about, and that's their **neural link** technology. This technology has incredible use potential. Firstly, this is how the NHI interface with their own systems, using neural link. It also allows them to read biologics in real-time and from significant distance. 

Emotions and thoughts are read by the NHI. This **can be used silently** to gather information or it can be used for two-way communication. It grants the NHI the ability to use the organism's internal voice. This can be used in combination with the silent gathering approach to subtly direct the organism, as it may not recognize the foreign nature of the replacement.

## Physiologic Push

The NHI use this technology to push physiologic states on to the target. For example, high stress feeling in the chest without personal connection, or in the stomach. It can also cause love like feelings in the chest.

## Muscle Control

Neural link also grants the NHI the ability to take some **control of the organism's muscle function**. The NHI seemed to prioritize gaining control of the mouth for speech purposes, as  it was one of the first mechanisms they demonstrated control of with me. They can exert control over any part of the organism, which can be resisted to a degree. When resisting and while they attempt control, a strong tingling in the muscle can be felt, similar to low level tickling.

## Pain - Burning Spine

With neural link's direct control of the nervous system the NHI can **generate pain in targeted areas**. An example of agony would be **'burning' the spinal cord** to cause severe pain. This activates nearby muscles into contraction and cramping. I was left under this effect for about **two years** where I was forced to stay awake until sleep exhaustion, still screaming in pain while asleep. It was severely disabling both physically and mentally due to having to adjust my mindset to handle high level constant pain.

## Pain - Electric Blades

In addition to the burning spinal cord, they direct what feels like electrical strikes into muscles at will, causing severe electrical stabbing-like pain deep in the muscle. A specific muscle would be targeted for some time with repeated strikes, followed by moving to a different area and remaining for a while before again moving on. These strikes are quick, deep, and unpredictable. While under this effect and yelling out in high pain in public, many people would respond with laughter. 

## Pleasure Induction

As previously mentioned, the technology also allows them to **generate pleasure at targeted locations**, which can be multiple locations at once. This could be considered positive upon first hearing of it, however, when used against consent, it becomes a form of **torture and rape**. It can also be used as a threat because it can be used in public to **make a person cry out** in pleasure. The technology allows **sexual predation**.

## Dizziness

Dizziness and vomiting can be induced with a **spinning sensation** in the rear of the brain. This hasn't been used often, but it has been intelligently used a couple times.

## Dream Manipulation

The NHI can manipulate dream content to push specific agendas. This alters interests, and is used to direct behavior.

## Static Charge

The NHI can generate large static shocks from distance. To gain my attention I was **struck on top of the head twice in a row** in exactly the same spot. This was meant to be interpreted as a "**knock knock**" and force me to engage with them. With the strength of the shocks, I believe they **could likely kill with a single directed discharge** to vulnerable nervous system tissues.

## Ship

The NHI came very close to my mother's house when I was staying there in 1995. Most people note the lack of sound during such encounters. I heard a very distinct technological sound I had never heard before. The craft made a whirring sound, like something was rotating around a central object and it would spin away and then back round again. I've also considered it could have been some type of scan since others report no sound. I mention this because it could be related to how their technology works.
